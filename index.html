Visitor Essentials

Quick Travel Tips

  • Best Time to Visit: May through September for pleasant weather.
  • Currency: Kazakhstani Tenge (KZT). Cards are widely accepted.
  • Public Transport: Buses 10 and 12 connect the airport directly to downtown.

Tourist Information Kiosks

Official visitor kiosks are located inside Nursultan Nazarbayev International Airport (NQZ) and Nur-Sultan-1 Railway Station offering free bilingual city maps and assistance.
