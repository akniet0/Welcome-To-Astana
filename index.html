Welcome to Astana

Astana is a unique blend of futuristic architecture, rich nomadic heritage, and warm hospitality. Located in central Kazakhstan, the city stands out with iconic landmarks designed by world-renowned architects, expansive public parks, and vibrant cultural centers.

Quick Travel Tips

  1. Best time to visit: Late spring through early autumn (May to September).
  2. Currency: Kazakhstani Tenge (KZT). Card payments are widely accepted.
  3. Getting around: Easy access via city buses and ride-hailing apps.
