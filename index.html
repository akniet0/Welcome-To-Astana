Welcome to Astana

Astana is a unique blend of futuristic architecture, rich nomadic heritage, and warm hospitality. Located in central Kazakhstan, the city stands out with iconic landmarks designed by world-renowned architects, expansive public parks, and cultural centers.

Why Visit Astana?

Whether you are visiting for world-class architecture, traditional Kazakh cuisine, or lively festivals, Astana offers unforgettable experiences:

  • Iconic Architecture: Marvel at Baiterek Tower, Khan Shatyr, and the Nur-Alem sphere.
  • Cultural Centers: Explore the National Museum of Kazakhstan and the Astana Opera.
  • Green Spaces: Walk along the Ishim River embankment and Central Park.

Quick Travel Tips

  1. Best time to visit: Late spring through early autumn (May to September).
  2. Currency: Kazakhstani Tenge (KZT). Card payments are widely accepted.
  3. Getting around: Easy access via city buses, ride-hailing apps, and bicycle rentals.
