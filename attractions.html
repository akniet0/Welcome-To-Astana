Must-See Landmarks in Astana

Astana is world-renowned for its visionary skyline and monuments that reflect Kazakh identity, history, and architectural ambition.

1. Baiterek Tower

The symbol of the capital, Baiterek represents the mythical Tree of Life where the sacred bird Samruk laid a golden egg. Visitors can ascend to the panoramic observation deck for 360-degree views of the government district.

  • Height: 105 meters
  • Location: Nurzhol Boulevard
  • Best For: Skyline photography and city views

2. Khan Shatyr Entertainment Center

Designed by British architect Norman Foster, this is the world's largest tensile structure resembling a giant nomadic tent. Beyond retail shopping, it houses an indoor tropical beach resort with sand imported from the Maldives.

  • Architect: Norman Foster
  • Key Feature: Sky Beach Club and climate-controlled interior
  • Location: Turan Avenue

3. Hazret Sultan Mosque

One of the largest mosques in Central Asia, Hazret Sultan features classic oriental architecture intertwined with traditional Kazakh decorative motifs, white marble facades, and immense prayer halls.

  • Style: Traditional Islamic with Kazakh ornamental patterns
  • Capacity: Up to 10,000 worshippers
  • Location: Tauelsizdik Avenue

Recommended Walking Route

  1. Start early at the Hazret Sultan Mosque on the east axis.
  2. Stroll down Nurzhol Boulevard toward the center to visit Baiterek Tower.
  3. End your afternoon at Khan Shatyr for shopping, dining, and relaxation.
